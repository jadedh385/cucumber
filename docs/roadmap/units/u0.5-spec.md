# U0.5 · Authentication — Spec (G0)

**Unit:** [U0.5](../phase-0-foundations.md#u05--authentication) · **Size:** M ·
**Deps:** U0.3, U0.4a (both passed G6) · **Decisions:** none blocking (D-10 Resolved; D-03 belongs to U0.6)
**Status:** Draft · 2026-10-19 · waiting on [Owner answers](#owner-answers) Q1–Q5

## Goal

Give every staff member their own Cucumber account and a safe way in and out.
Cucumber has two locks:

1. **Cloudflare Access** (outer lock, U0.4b): an email code, asked again every
   30 days per phone, before anyone reaches Cucumber.
2. **Cucumber's own login** (inner lock, this unit): an email address and password
   tied to one person, so every saved entry names who saved it.

Today only Django's built-in login page is there (U0.3), and after logging in
it lands on a missing page. After U0.5, staff log in from a phone, stay
signed in for a sensible time, and log out. Every page except the login page
and `/health/` needs a signed-in user. The Owner can add staff, switch them
off, and give them a new password when they forget it. U0.6 (roles), U0.7
(audit trail) and U0.10 (app shell) build on this.

## User stories

| # | As a… | I want… | So that… |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Staff member | to log in on my phone with my email and password | my entries are saved under my name |
| S2 | Staff member | to stay signed in while I work, without typing my password at every bench | data entry stays quick |
| S3 | Staff member | to log out, and to change my own password | nobody else uses my account on my phone |
| S4 | Owner | to add a staff account and give the person a first password | new hires can start the same day |
| S5 | Owner | to switch an account off when someone leaves, at once | they can't get in, and their past entries stay |
| S6 | Owner | to give a staff member a new password when they forget theirs | nobody is stuck, and no email set-up is needed |
| S7 | Owner | guessing passwords to be stopped | a weak password can't be found by trying many times |
| S8 | Owner | the login page not to show whether an email has an account | nobody can find out who works here by trying emails |
| S9 | Owner | a way back in if I lock myself out | the business never loses its only Admin |

## Acceptance criteria

The numbers in square brackets come from the [recommended answers](#owner-answers);
they change if the Owner picks another option.

**Logging in**

- **AC1** *Given* an active account, *when* the right email and password are entered, *then* the user lands on Cucumber's home page (`LOGIN_REDIRECT_URL`), not Django's `/accounts/profile/` (U0.3 G4).
- **AC2** *Given* a wrong password, an email with no account, or a deactivated account, *when* someone logs in, *then* each gets the **same** message ("Email or password is wrong.") and the same response, so nobody can tell which it was. An unknown email takes about as long to check as a known one.
- **AC3** *Given* an email typed with capital letters or spaces around it, *then* it matches the account the same as the plain lowercase email ([Q1](#owner-answers)).
- **AC4** *Given* a login link with `?next=//evil.example` or `?next=https://evil.example`, *when* the user logs in, *then* they land on the home page, never on another site. A `next` that points inside Cucumber works (U0.1 spike review).
- **AC5** *Given* a login form sent without a valid CSRF token, *then* it is refused. The test turns CSRF checks **on** (`enforce_csrf_checks`): Django's test client skips them by default (U0.1 spike review).
- **AC6** *Given* a phone 375 px wide, *when* the login page opens, *then* it fits without sideways scrolling, the email box opens the email keyboard, password managers can fill it, and it shows the "Practice data" banner on staging. All its text goes through the translation function (D-16). It replaces U0.3's stopgap page.

**Too many wrong passwords** ([Q3](#owner-answers))

- **AC7** *Given* [5] wrong passwords for the same email within [15 minutes], *then* that email is locked for [15 minutes]: every try, even with the right password, gets "Too many tries. Wait [15] minutes or ask the Owner." The wrong passwords themselves are never stored or logged.
- **AC8** *Given* the same number of tries for an email with **no** account, *then* it locks in exactly the same way, so locking doesn't show whether the account exists.
- **AC9** *Given* staff on the lab Wi-Fi share one internet address, *when* one person locks their own email, *then* others on the same network can still log in. A second, looser limit per network address ([50] tries in [15 minutes]) stops one device trying many emails.
- **AC10** *Given* the app runs two worker processes and can restart, *then* the count and the lock are the same in every worker and survive a restart (kept in the database, not in one worker's memory).
- **AC11** *Given* a locked email, *then* the Admin can lift the lock early ([Q4](#owner-answers)), and a good login after the wait starts the count again from zero.

**Staying signed in and logging out** ([Q2](#owner-answers))

- **AC12** *Given* a signed-in user who opens no page for [7 days], *then* their session ends and the next page sends them to the login page.
- **AC13** *Given* a user who keeps using Cucumber every day, *then* their session still ends [30 days] after they logged in.
- **AC14** *Given* a signed-in user, *when* they press Log out, *then* the session ends. Log out works only as a form submit (POST); opening the logout address as a link (GET) is refused and logs no one out (U0.1 spike review).
- **AC15** *Given* a login, *then* the user gets a new session id (an id planted before login is useless after it), and the session cookie can't be read by page scripts, is sent only over HTTPS on servers, and isn't sent with requests started from other sites (`SameSite=Lax`).
- **AC16** *Given* sessions that have ended, *then* they are removed from the database at least once a week.

**Every page needs a login**

- **AC17** *Given* any web address in Cucumber, *then* it needs a signed-in user, except a short allow-list: the login page, `/health/`, and CSS / images. A test walks **every** URL pattern, so a new page can't be left open by accident.
- **AC18** *Given* someone not signed in, *when* they open a page, *then* they go to the login page and, after logging in, back to that page. `/` opens the home page when signed in and the login page when not (U0.4b follow-up: today `/` gives "Not Found").
- **AC19** *Given* a session that has ended, *when* an HTMX request arrives (a form on the page saving in the background), *then* the answer is **401** with `HX-Redirect` to the login page, so the whole page goes to login. The login page is never swapped into the form (U0.1 spike review).
- **AC20** *Given* someone not signed in, *when* they send a save, *then* it is refused and no row is written (U0.1 spike review).
- **AC21** *Given* a page for signed-in users, *then* it uses the base page U0.5 builds: Cucumber's name, who is signed in, Log out, and the "Practice data" banner on staging (U0.4a §4.4). Navigation and the full UI kit come in U0.10.

**Staff accounts** ([Q4](#owner-answers), [Q5](#owner-answers))

- **AC22** *Given* the Admin, *when* they add an account with a name, an email and a first password, *then* the person can log in. An email can't be used by two accounts (capitals ignored). A new account has no role until U0.6, so it sees only the home page.
- **AC23** *Given* the Admin, *when* they deactivate an account, *then* the person can't log in (AC2 message), and a session they already have open ends on its next page. The account and every entry it saved stay; the Admin can reactivate it. Accounts are never deleted (U0.2 design §2.7).
- **AC24** *Given* the Admin, *then* they can't deactivate their own account, so Cucumber always keeps an Admin. (U0.6 adds the full "last Admin" rule for roles.)
- **AC25** *Given* the Admin, *when* they give a staff member a new password, *then* every open session of that person ends, and at the next login the person must choose their own new password before any other page opens.
- **AC26** *Given* a signed-in user, *when* they change their own password (typing the current one first), *then* their other sessions end and this one stays signed in.
- **AC27** *Given* any new password, first or reset ones included, *then* it follows Django's four rules, already switched on: at least 8 characters, not a common password, not only numbers, not too like the person's name or email.
- **AC28** *Given* a user who isn't the Admin, *when* they open an account page or send one of its forms, *then* it is refused and nothing changes. (U0.6 builds the full role-by-page matrix; U0.5 checks only "is Admin", with the existing `has_role` helper.)
- **AC29** *Given* a server with no Admin account yet, or an Owner who is locked out or forgot their password, *then* a command run on the server over SSH creates the first Admin or sets the Owner's new password, under the same password rules. This is how production gets its first account at G6.
- **AC30** *Given* a login, a failed login, a lock, a logout, or an account change (added, deactivated, reactivated, password reset, lock lifted), *then* one plain line goes to the app log: what happened, to which account, by whom, when. A password or session id is never written. U0.7 moves these into the audit trail.

**Database**

- **AC31** *Given* the app's database user `cucumber_app`, *then* it can change only the `auth_user` columns the app needs (password, last login, active, name, email, username). Changing `is_superuser` or `is_staff` as `cucumber_app` fails at the database (U0.2a G4 carry-forward). It still can't delete a user.

## Out of scope

- **Roles and the permission matrix** (U0.6), including giving and removing
  roles. U0.5 knows only "is this user the Admin".
- **The audit trail table** (U0.7). U0.5 writes log lines (AC30); U0.7 moves them in.
  Staff anonymisation on request (PDPA) is also U0.7.
- **Two-step login (2FA)** (U6.1) and **rate limits on every page** (U6.6). D-10
  keeps both in Phase 6 while Access guards the app. U0.5 limits login only.
- **Password reset by email link.** Cucumber sends no email. The Owner gives a
  new password instead ([Q5](#owner-answers)).
- **Sign in with Google or Microsoft**, and a "remember me" box.
- **Checking Cloudflare's signed Access header inside the app.** Left out in
  [U0.4b design §7](u0.4b-design.md); revisit only if the app is ever reachable
  without Access.
- **The app shell** (U0.10): menus, search, the daisyUI kit, the CSRF-failure
  page. U0.5 builds only the plain base page (AC21) and an empty home page.
- **Django's admin site.** It isn't installed and stays out. It's built for
  desktops, and its edits would skip Cucumber's own rules.
- **Copying accounts between staging and production.** Staging has its own
  practice accounts.

## Size

**M.** No new tables (Django's `auth_user` and `django_session` exist; the
lock counts need one small table or a library's tables, chosen at G1). Work:
login and logout pages, the "login needed everywhere" check, the lock, session
timing, a few Admin account pages (or commands, Q4), one server command, the
column grants, and the base and home pages. If Q4 is b), it's a small M.
